## ARPANET 
**ARPANET** stands for **Advanced Research Projects Agency Network** — and it’s basically the **grandfather of the internet**.
- It was created in **1969** by the ARPA (a U.S. government agency).
- Its goal was to connect computers from different universities and research centers.
- Before ARPANET, computers were isolated — they couldn’t easily “talk” to each other.
ARPANET introduced **Packet Switching** (data is broken into small packets and sent separately).
