## Broken Authentication/Access Control

Refers to _vulnerabilities_ that allow attackers to bypass authentication functions, and access or being more previlged to do thing that normally they are not in his permission.

### Broken Authentication: 
Simply are vulnerablities that makes the attacker able to authenticate/login without having a valide set of credentials/ or allow a normal user to gain admin control and previllege.

### Broken Access Control
Vulnerablities that allow to attacker to access features and pages that have no permission to do so.
